About the Creators

This website was created to give people a simple and engaging introduction to East and West Coast hip-hop. Many listeners know the rivalry, but not everyone understands the artists that shaped each coast. Our site highlights important artists from both sides and offers a discover page to explore even more music. By making this website, we wanted to encourage others to learn more about the history, culture, and influence of both coasts as well as hip-hop beyond what they already know.
