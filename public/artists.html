Tupac (2Pac) Shakur

Name/s: Tupac Amaru Shakur (full stage name)
Lesane Parish Crooks (full birth name)
2Pac (stage name)
Makaveli (stage name)

Birth Date: June 16, 1971

Birth Place: Manhattan, New York City, U.S.A.

Date of Death: September 13, 1996


Tupac was an American rapper, actor, and poet known for his powerful lyrics about racism, poverty, violence, and social injustice. He became one of the most influential and iconic figures in hip-hop. Despite his short life, his music and activism left a lasting global impact.


Tupac produced some of the most enduring hits in hip-hop, blending raw emotion with social commentary. Songs like “California Love,” “Changes,” “Hit ’Em Up,” “Dear Mama,” “Keep Ya Head Up,” “Hail Mary,” and “All Eyez on Me” showcased his versatility—from party anthems to deeply personal reflections. His music often addressed themes of inequality, resilience, and loyalty, resonating with listeners across generations. These hits cemented his legacy as one of the most influential artists in rap history.

Snoop Doggy Dogg

Name/s: Snoop Doggy Dogg (full stage name)
Calvin Cordozar Broadus Jr. (full birth name)
Snoop Dogg (stage name)

Birth Date: October 20, 1971

Birth Place: Long Beach, California, U.S.A.

Date of Death: N/A


Snoop Dogg is an American rapper, singer, actor, and cultural icon who rose to fame in the early 1990s under Dr. Dre’s mentorship. Known for his laid-back flow and West Coast sound, he became one of hip-hop’s most recognizable figures. Throughout his long career, he has remained influential in music, pop culture, and entertainment.


Snoop Dogg has delivered some of hip-hop’s most iconic hits, marked by his smooth flow and unmistakable West Coast style. His breakout tracks like “Gin and Juice,” “Who Am I? (What’s My Name?),” and his features on “Nuthin’ but a ‘G’ Thang” and “Still D.R.E.” helped define 1990s rap. Later songs such as “Drop It Like It’s Hot,” “Beautiful,” and “Young, Wild & Free” showed his ability to stay relevant across generations. These hits highlight his versatility and cement his status as one of rap’s most enduring and influential artists.

Dr. Dre

Name/s: Dr. Dre (full stage name)
Andre Romell Young (full birth name)

Birth Date: February 18, 1965

Birth Place: Compton, California, U.S.A.

Date of Death: N/A


Dr. Dre is an American rapper, producer, and entrepreneur who helped shape West Coast hip-hop. He co-founded N.W.A, launched artists like Snoop Dogg and Eminem, and became one of the most influential producers in music history.




Dr. Dre has a plethora of hit songs, beginning with N.W.A.'s groundbreaking tracks like "Express Yourself". His solo career launched with the G-funk defining album The Chronic, which featured major hits such as the Grammy-winning "Let Me Ride" and the number two Billboard single "Nuthin' but a 'G' Thang" (featuring Snoop Dogg). He continued his success with the album 2001, producing iconic songs like "Still D.R.E." and "Forgot About Dre" (both featuring Eminem), which became top ten hits on the rap charts. His production work for other artists also yielded massive hits, including 2Pac's "California Love," Eminem's "My Name Is," and 50 Cent's "In Da Club".

Eazy-E

Name/s: Eazy-E (full stage name)
Eric Lynn Wright (full birth name)

Birth Date: September 7, 1964

Birth Place: Compton, California, U.S.A.

Date of Death: March 26, 1995


Eazy-E was an American rapper and record executive, a founding member of N.W.A, and a key figure in gangsta rap. Known for his raw delivery and street realism, he played a major role in bringing West Coast hip-hop into the mainstream.


Eazy-E is best known for hits that defined early gangsta rap and the West Coast sound. Songs like **“Boyz-n-the-Hood,” “Eazy-Duz-It,” “We Want Eazy,”** and **“Real Muthaphuckkin G’s”** showcased his raw, unapologetic style and street-focused storytelling. As part of N.W.A, he also contributed to landmark tracks such as **“Straight Outta Compton”** and **“Gangsta Gangsta.”** These hits helped shape hip-hop’s direction in the late 1980s and early 1990s and cemented Eazy-E’s legacy as a rap pioneer.

Jay-Z (stage name)

Birth Date: December 4, 1969

Jay-Z is an American rapper, songwriter, and entrepreneur known for his lyrical skill and business success. Rising from Brooklyn’s rap scene, he became one of the most influential figures in hip-hop and a major force in music, fashion, and entertainment.




Jay-Z has released numerous hit songs that showcase his lyrical depth and long-lasting influence in hip-hop. His music blends storytelling, confidence, and social insight, allowing him to stay relevant across decades. These hits helped solidify Jay-Z’s reputation as one of the greatest and most successful rappers of all time.

The Notorious B.I.G. released several iconic hits that left a lasting mark on hip-hop. Nas is an American rapper and songwriter celebrated for his storytelling, lyricism, and social commentary. Emerging in the early 1990s with his debut album Illmatic, he became one of hip-hop’s most respected and influential artists.

50 Cent

Name/s: 50 Cent (full stage name)
Curtis James Jackson III (full birth name)

Birth Date: July 6, 1975

Birth Place: Queens, New York City, U.S.A.

Date of Death: N/A


50 Cent is an American rapper, actor, and entrepreneur known for his aggressive rap style and street-inspired lyrics. He rose to fame with his debut album Get Rich or Die Tryin’ and became one of the most influential figures in early 2000s hip-hop.


50 Cent is known for a string of hits that defined early 2000s hip-hop. Songs like **“In Da Club,” “21 Questions,” “P.I.M.P.,” “Candy Shop,”** and **“Many Men (Wish Death)”** showcased his catchy hooks, gritty storytelling, and street-smart persona. These hits not only topped charts worldwide but also solidified 50 Cent’s reputation as one of the most influential and commercially successful rappers of his era.
